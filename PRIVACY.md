# Privacy Policy: Hold to Speed Up for TikTok

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Hold to Speed Up for TikTok ("the extension") does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

## What the extension stores

Only your own settings, saved with Chrome's built-in `chrome.storage.sync`:

- the speed used while holding (1.5x, 2x, 2.5x or 3x)
- the optional hold key you chose
- whether the extension is switched on
- the list of sites you switched it off on

Chrome syncs these settings between your own browsers if you have Chrome sync turned on. The developer cannot see them.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not read, record or send page content, browsing history, clicks or keystrokes.
- It makes no network requests and loads no remote code, fonts or images.
- It has no analytics, tracking or ads.

The content script runs on web pages only so it can find the video under your cursor and change its playback speed while you hold.
